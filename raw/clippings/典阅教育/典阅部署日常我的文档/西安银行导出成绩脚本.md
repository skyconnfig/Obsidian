# 西安银行导出成绩脚本

```python
SELECT
	             t1.*
	             FROM exam_theoryassessmenttotalresult as t1 inner join system_userinfo as t2 on t1.tellerID=t2.id 
	             inner join question_theoryassessment as t3 on t1.theoryassessmentID=t3.id 
	             inner join question_theoryassessment_detail as t4 on t4.theoryassessmentID=t3.id 
				 inner join question_theoryexam as t5 on t5.id=t4.theoryExamID 
	             where t2.userType=2 and (select count(-1) from question_theoryassessment where id=t1.theoryassessmentID)>0 
                 and (t3.isSecret is null or t3.isSecret=0 or t3.addOperator='admin') 
                 and (t5.isprivate is null or t5.isprivate=0 or t5.addOperator='admin') 
	             and t3.isSuccess=0 
	          and t1.addTime>='2025-09-25'
	            ORDER BY totalScore desc 
```
